## case-01-common-mistakes.md

1. "Its essential" → "It's essential" (it's vs its).
2. "associated to the MAO" → "associated with" (associated with vs to).
3. "Once you save" → "After you save" (once is not a synonym for after).
4. "Ten Business Data entities or less" → "or fewer" (fewer vs less).
5. "You can only select one entry" → "You can select only one entry" (misplacement of only).
6. "sign-in" as verb → "sign in"; "sign in screen" as noun/adjective → "sign-in screen" (verb vs noun form).
7. "The id" → "The ID" (ID vs Id/id).
8. "enviroment" → "environment"; "bellow" → "below" (misspellings).
