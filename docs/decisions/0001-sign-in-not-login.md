# 0001 — Use "sign in", not "login", as the verb

- **Date:** 2026-08-30
- **Status:** accepted

## Decision

Use "sign in" / "log in" as verbs and "sign-in" / "login" only as nouns or adjectives. Prefer the "sign in" family in new content.

## Why

The style guide already distinguished verb and noun forms; writers still mixed them. Fixing the verb/noun split (and preferring one family) removes a recurring review comment.

## Consequences

Glossary entry added; the style-guide-validator flags the verb "login". Existing pages are corrected opportunistically, not in bulk.
