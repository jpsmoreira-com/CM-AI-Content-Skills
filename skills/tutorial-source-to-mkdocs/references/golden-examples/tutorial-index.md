# Manage Example Orders

This tutorial shows how to create, track, and complete an example order in Critical Manufacturing MES.

## Who this is for

Production operators and supervisors who manage orders daily.

## Prerequisites

- Access to a Critical Manufacturing MES environment.
- A **Facility** and at least one **Area** already configured.
