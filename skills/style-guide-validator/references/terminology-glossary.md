# Terminology Glossary

Approved terminology for Critical Manufacturing MES documentation. The style guide governs wording and formatting; this glossary governs *which term to use*. Entries are alphabetical. When the glossary and a portal-local rule conflict, the portal rule wins for that portal — and should be proposed back here.

> Seed content: extend this table as terms come up. One PR per term or small group of terms, with the "why" filled in.

## Product and brand names

| Use | Never | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Critical Manufacturing MES | CMF MES, the MES, cmNavigo | Full name on first use per page; "MES" alone only after first use. |
| Critical Manufacturing | CMF, CM | In prose. Internal abbreviations stay internal. |

## Approved vs. forbidden terms

| Use | Instead of | Why |
| --- | --- | --- |
| sign in (verb), sign-in (noun/adjective) | login (verb), log-in | Verb vs. noun form; see decision record 0001. |
| ID | Id, id | Style guide: consistency. |
| email | e-mail, E-mail | Style guide: do not capitalize; no hyphen. |
| associated with | associated to | Standard English; style guide "Common Mistakes". |
| after | once (as time connective) | "Once" is not a synonym for "after". |
| because | since (causal) | Words with one clear meaning. |
| select | click on, press (for UI) | Device-neutral instruction verb. |

## MES entities

Capitalize and bold Critical Manufacturing MES entities in technical documents (Material, Facility, Area, Flow, Step, Checklist, Transfer Order). Match GUI spelling exactly. If a definition-page hyperlink is available, the hyperlink replaces the bold.
